V18 - Gameplay Simulation Professional Update

1. AI Tactical Adaptation
- AI sekarang membaca skor, menit, kartu merah, stamina rata-rata, kondisi unggul/tertinggal.
- Saat tertinggal di menit akhir, AI menaikkan attack bias, risk pass, dan shooting urgency.
- Saat unggul, AI menurunkan risiko, menjaga shape, dan lebih memilih safe pass/carry aman.
- Saat stamina rendah atau kalah jumlah pemain, pressing dan risk otomatis turun.

2. Player Role Behavior
- Setiap role punya intent berbeda:
  GK: safe distribution, no risky dribble.
  CB: pass aman, duel, minim shooting.
  Fullback: width/carry/cross lane.
  CDM/CM: tempo pass dan support.
  CAM: through ball dan creator.
  Winger: carry wide, cut inside, progressive pass.
  ST: shot priority dan central movement.

3. Shot Quality System
- Shot chance sekarang membaca angle, balance, composure, pressure, role intent, stamina, dan weak-foot/arah.
- Log shot menampilkan quality: poor/ok/good/elite.
- Spread tembakan lebih kecil jika quality bagus, lebih liar jika body balance buruk.

4. Passing Intelligence
- AI memilih safe pass/through ball berdasarkan style, skor, pressure, risk lane, role passer, role receiver, dan urgency.
- Protect mode lebih menghargai pass aman.
- Chase mode lebih berani through ball/progressive pass.

5. Dribble Intelligence
- AI dribble tidak random: membaca nearest defender, lane home, role width, role carry, score state, dan style.
- Winger lebih natural melebar, ST/CAM lebih natural mencari tengah.

6. Fairness Test Harness
- Ditambahkan src/game/fairnessTest.js.
- Bisa dipakai untuk batch test 1000 match: home win rate, away win rate, draw rate, upset rate, avg goals.

Catatan:
- Patch ini fokus gameplay simulation/AI behavior tanpa mengubah save data lama.
- Existing V17 fairness tetap dipertahankan: kickoff babak 1 acak, babak 2 bergantian, foul restart, possession per detik, smarter tackle, trajectory keeper save.
